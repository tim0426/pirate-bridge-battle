# Pirate Bridge Battle / 海盜獨木橋對戰

Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `python3 -m http.server` from this `game/` folder and visit the local URL. Works with touch.

**Player vs Bot (PvZ-style).** You play the left pirate and place units on your half. The computer (navy) sends units down the 3 bridges toward your ship. Tap a card, then a green cell. Tap **ADVANCE** to fight, walk, and earn 2 gold (max 12).

**Ranged stays on your half and can only shoot up to the midline.** Cannon can still strike the enemy half. First ship to 0 HP loses.
